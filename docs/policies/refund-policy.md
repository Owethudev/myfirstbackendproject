# Refund policy

**Status:** current-product statement; review before adding payments
**Version:** 1.0
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12

## Current payment status

The current application has no checkout, paid subscription, or in-app payment integration. It does not request payment through the app, so there are no app purchases to refund under the current implementation.

This statement is limited to the application's code and does not cover separate arrangements made outside the service.

## If a payment feature is introduced

Before accepting payment, the operator must publish clear pricing, identify all mandatory fees and taxes before confirmation, describe cancellation and refund eligibility and timelines, provide a support contact, and ensure the process complies with applicable consumer law. Do not launch a paid flow while these details are missing.

## Contact

No billing contact is configured in this repository. The operator must provide a monitored contact method before any payment feature is offered.
